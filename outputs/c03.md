You have up to 30 days to return most items bought online. The Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 give you a cancellation period that runs from the day the item is delivered, and you must tell the retailer you are cancelling before it ends. You then have 14 days to send the item back. The retailer must refund you within 14 days of receiving the goods or proof of return.

This does not cover personalised items or sealed goods that have been opened. Check the retailer's returns policy before ordering.
