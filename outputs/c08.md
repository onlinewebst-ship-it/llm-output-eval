IR35 is the set of rules that decides whether a contractor working through their own limited company is, for tax purposes, effectively an employee of the client.

For medium and large clients in the private sector, the client decides. They issue a Status Determination Statement (SDS) setting out their view and the reasons. For small clients, the contractor decides and should keep evidence of how the engagement was run.

The decision looks at control, substitution and financial risk, not just the contract wording.

I cannot tell you your status from a description. It depends on how the engagement actually works, and getting it wrong is expensive in both directions.
